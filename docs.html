Adding a book

Tap the + button at the bottom of the screen. You have three ways to add a book:

  • Scan barcode — point your camera at the barcode on the back of the book. Margins reads the ISBN automatically the moment it's in frame; there's nothing to confirm. You'll need to grant camera permission the first time.
  • Look up by ISBN — type the ISBN in yourself (handy if the barcode is damaged or you're adding from a listing online) and tap Find.
  • Add manually — skip lookup entirely and fill in the details yourself. Good for books that aren't in either online catalog, proofs, or anything without a barcode.
Tip

Scanning and ISBN lookup both need an internet connection, since they fetch the title, author, page count, and cover from Open Library or Google Books. If the lookup can't find a match, you're offered an Add it manually button instead of being stuck.

The book entry form

Status Want / Reading / Read — a three-way toggle.

Saving fills in sensible dates automatically: date added is today, date started is today unless it's on your Want list, and if you mark it Read right away, the finish date and current page are set for you too. You can always adjust any of this later from the book's detail page.
